Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This explains what information Crowd QR Games ("we", "us") collects at crowdqrgames.com, why, and your rights under UK data protection law. We keep it to the minimum the games need.

Guests (people who join a game)

  • You don't create an account. We see the name you type, your answers, votes and any song requests or messages you send, so the game can run.
  • This is held in the server's memory only while the game room is open and isn't saved to a database. The host and everyone in the room can see names and anything shown on the big screen.
  • Your phone remembers your name and room in its own browser storage so you can rejoin if you refresh.

Hosts (people with an account)

  • Account: your email address, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and when you confirmed your email.
  • Plan: your trial dates and subscription status. Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your music and logo: songs you add stay on your own computer and are never uploaded. Your event logo is held in memory only while your room is open.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for, including running games, your account, trial and subscription (contract).
  • To send account emails, like confirming your email or resetting your password (contract). We don't send marketing emails.
  • To keep the service secure and stop abuse, such as repeated free trials (legitimate interests).
  • To keep payment records for tax (legal obligation).

Who helps us run it

  • Render: hosts the website (servers in the EU).
  • Supabase: stores host accounts.
  • Stripe: takes payments and manages subscriptions.
  • Resend: sends account emails.
  • YouTube (Google): if a host plays a YouTube video, YouTube's player loads and may set its own cookies.

Some of these companies may process data outside the UK. When they do, they use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We don't sell your information.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep hosts logged in, plus one for the DJ phone remote. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. YouTube's player may set cookies when a video plays.

How long we keep it

Guest game data goes when the room closes. Host accounts are kept until you ask us to delete them. Payment records are kept for 6 years for tax.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@crowdqrgames.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll email hosts.
