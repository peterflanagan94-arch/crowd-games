Eight games, ready to go

Quiz

Timed questions with points for speed and a live leaderboard. Use your own questions or the ready-made packs.

Emoji guess

Work out the film, song or saying from the emojis. Disney, 80s, 90s and 2000s hits, Christmas, musicals and more.

Music bingo

Every card is different and full of song titles. Play the tunes and guests tap what they hear.

Bingo

Classic 75-number bingo. The app checks every claim, so false calls get caught.

Higher or lower

A real shuffled deck. Higher or lower? Aces high, low or either.

Mr & Mrs Weddings

The couple answer in secret, then the guests guess what they said. Reveal the couple, then the crowd.

Volunteer draw

Hula hoop-offs, fancy-dress karaoke, dance-offs. Guests tap "I'm in!" and the app picks at random, with a drumroll.

Crowd vote

"What should I play next?" or "Who's most likely to…?" with live results on screen.

How it works

1

Open the host screen

Put it on the projector or TV and give your night a title. Wedding mode adds the couple's names.

2

Guests scan the QR code

Their phone becomes the controller. Nothing to install, and up to hundreds of players.

3

Run it from the decks

Press space to move on, or use the phone remote so you never leave the booth.
